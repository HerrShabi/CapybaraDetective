# CLAUDE.md

SUPER CAPYBARA: POLICE DETECTIVE: Ethan's (age 9) 4th game, after SuperCapybara (`../SuperCapybara`), SupaCapy 2 (`../SupaCapy2`) and SuperCapybara 3 (`../SuperCapybara3`). Made with his dad (Raz). Talk to Ethan in short, simple, friendly language; ask him the creative choices one at a time (lettered options A/B/C, always with your own pick and why: Ethan wants your opinion every time), make the technical ones yourself. Build after every 10th question or sooner if he asks, and write each decision into `GAME_DESIGN.md` right away. **Only commit or push when Ethan's dad asks** .

**Going online (Raz, 2026-11-22):** the repo is `git@github.com:HerrShabi/CapybaraDetective.git`, and it's set up like SuperCapybara 3: `npm run build` makes `dist/` for `https://herrshabi.github.io/CapybaraDetective/` (base path in `vite.config.js`: keep it the same as the repo name, it's case-sensitive), with the home-screen manifest (`public/manifest.webmanifest`), icons (`public/icons/`), and the offline service worker `sw.js` (the `offline()` plugin, registered in `main.js` in production). `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` publishes every push to `main` (the repo's Settings → Pages → Source must be "GitHub Actions"). **Only commit or push when Ethan's dad asks.** `npx vite preview` (port 4176) + `PLAYTEST_URL=http://localhost:4176/CapybaraDetective/` tests the built app. The game's solved cases are in localStorage, so the installed app has its own, separate from the browser's.
- App icon: drawn in `scripts/icon.html`; to remake the PNGs, screenshot it with headless Chrome at 512x512 into `public/icons/icon-512.png`, then `sips -z 192 192` → `icon-192.png` and `sips -z 180 180` → `apple-touch-icon.png`.

- `npm run dev`: http://localhost:5176 (5173 SuperCapybara, 5174 SuperCapybara 3, 5175 SupaCapy 2). `npm run dev:ipad` also opens it on the home Wi-Fi.
- `node scripts/playtest.mjs '<steps>'`: headless Chrome playtest (see the top of the script; `S.` = `window.game`, the `Game`). Screenshots go to `/tmp/detective-<name>.png`. Look at them before saying something works. The opening needs: a key (starts the typing), ~3.6 s, ENTER (starts the case).
- Three.js + Vite, plain JS, no files for art or sound (everything is drawn/made in code).
  - `src/main.js`: the flow. Title (`showOpening`, with CASE buttons; `progress.js` keeps the solved cases in localStorage, case 2 unlocks after case 1) → case 1: `Game(case1)` → `Chase` → `Party(1)`; case 2: `Game(town2)` → `Game(sewer2)` → `Game(factory2)` → `Party(2)`. Each part makes its own renderer and HUD and takes them down when done. In dev, `window.game` is the current part and `window.detective` has `case1()`, `case2()`, `title()`.
  - `src/opening.js`: Ethan's opening (black screen types "STEALING DU", the poster slams at "DU"); the poster is the title screen (SVG detective picture, police tape, language and CASE buttons).
  - `src/game.js`: the engine that plays one **level** (the list of what a level can have is at the top of the file: `build`, `start`, `heroes`, `dark`, `clues`, `people`, `things`, `triggers`, `talk`, `suspects`/`thief`/`enoughProof`/`accused`, `intro`, `update`, `opened`...). Third-person camera (moves in front of `world.walls`), ← → turn, ↑ ↓ walk, circle-vs-box bumping against `world.solids`. Q = `switchHero()`, the other hero follows. `nearby()` + `canDo()`: the detective does clues and talking, Copybara doors, and `things` say `who`. `say(lines, after)` for talks, `solved(delay)` ends the level, `addHero()` (Copybara bursting into the factory), `removePerson()`, `later()` for timers.
  - `src/cases/case1.js`, `src/cases/case2.js`: the cases as levels (texts in i18n: `clue.<id>.*`, `talk.<person>.*`, `c2.*`).
  - `src/museum.js` (case 1), `src/town.js`, `src/sewer.js` (the seeded maze, its pizza-smell trail along the right way), `src/factory.js` (the dough machine `pourDough()`, the cage): the places. `buildMuseum`/`buildTown` take `{ solved: true }` for the parties.
  - `src/chase.js`: case 1's police car chase (phases drive → harbor → swim → caught → jail). The world runs along -z: the road (`ROAD_LEN`, `LANES`, `obstacles`), the dock (`DOCK_END`) and the water. First person: the car's inside (`makeInterior`) and the swimming paws (`makePaddlers`) are children of the camera. The siren makes `pulled` cars move to the side. Baby Capybara's radio lines are `radio.<id>` (each once), SuperCapybara's tips `tip.<kind>`.
  - `src/party.js`: the end-of-case party (`PARTIES[1]` the museum medal party, `PARTIES[2]` the pizza party).
  - `src/models.js` (heroes, case 1 people), `src/models2.js` (case 2: Chef Mozzarella, Pizza Boss and his disguise, Chef Pesto, Danny Dough, Waiter Mouse, the scooter, pizza robots): 3D characters from simple shapes, like SuperCapybara's 3D heroes (`../SuperCapybara/src/world2/models.js`).
  - `src/hud.js`: the HTML HUD: clue counter (opens the notebook), who you are (switch), prompt, the talk box (types the text), notebook, line-up, the big end screen, and iPad controls (joystick → `hud.stick`, X and 🔄 buttons; shown on touch screens).
  - `src/audio.js`: Web Audio sounds (`audio.play(name)`) and the sneaky detective song (`audio.music(true)`). Sound starts after the first key/tap.
  - `src/i18n.js`: every word the player sees, English and German (`t('key')`); the language is kept in localStorage (`detective.language`).
- Ethan's decisions: `GAME_DESIGN.md`. His drawings go in `concept-art/` (look at them before drawing something he drew).
